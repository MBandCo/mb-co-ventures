Our Story

How we got here

MB & Co Ventures was founded on a simple premise: good software is hard to find, and most of it was probably built by someone else's brother. We figured we could do better. The jury is still out, but so far nothing has caught fire.

We are a small, focused company. We take on work we believe in, build things we're proud of, and try not to overcomplicate either of those things.
